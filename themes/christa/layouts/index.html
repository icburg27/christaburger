The Work

Most organizations are not governing risk.
They are hoping policies will behave like systems.

A policy nobody reads is not a control. A framework nobody can execute under pressure is not governance. Real governance is the architecture through which an organization decides — consistently, accountably, and fast enough to matter — and it has to survive the day the assumptions break. That is the discipline I have spent two decades building, in environments where the consequences are real.
